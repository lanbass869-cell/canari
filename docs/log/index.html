What a passing proof means

Each entry commits to the exact bytes and SHA-256 of a published dataset (mcpcost, CC BY 4.0). The tree head signature proves who logged it; the inclusion proof proves the entry is in the log; the consistency proof proves a later head still contains an earlier one. None of that says the underlying data is correct -- only that the record was not added, dropped, or rewritten after the fact.
